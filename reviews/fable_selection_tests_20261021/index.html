    Fable review: selection-aware score and a Gaia search for the 25-deg second branch (2026-10-21)¶

    Claude verified: on-tube (|dphi2-med|<3 deg, |dPM|<2) / second-branch (pmra < member median - 3) debris fractions per 4-deg phi1 bin 6-22: 12.5 deg 0.56-0.70 / 0.12-0.18; 25 deg 0.39-0.47 / 0.28-0.38; bin/streams/m5_independent_selection.py (ABCD background method) exists.

    Findings¶

    • The 12.5-vs-25 gap (1071) is mostly debris off the members' tube, not track shape. First-order tube normalisation gives the 25-deg model back +805 (S = +-2 MAD), +684 (3), +595 (4), +398 (6 MAD); 60-70% from phi1 18-28.
    • The branch occupies the same region in both models (amplitude differs): d 4.0-4.6 kpc (track 5.0-5.4), dphi2 -4..-9 deg south of the track (only 10-19% overlaps the members' sky), pmra 5-6 mas/yr more negative, pmdec within ~1, v_los 30-60 km/s lower at phi1 18-27; 24% below the data box (dphi2 < -9.5).
    • Member catalogue (Ibata+2024 MRT) has no membership probability or rejected candidates.

    Test 2 (ranked first): Gaia DR3 search¶

    Region A per 4-deg phi1 bin from the model's branch PMs (e.g. phi1 6-10: pmra -14.3..-9.1, pmdec -10.2..-8.0; ... 18-22: -17.1..-14.2, -11.6..-10.2), dphi2 -13..-1 excluding +-2.5 deg of the track, parallax soft cut (0.18-0.27 incl. ZP), members' CMD locus shifted ~0.4 mag brighter (fit the shift), G 16-19.8, ruwe < 1.4, dereddened. One WSDB pull, selections in Python. Background ABCD: PM sidebands -5 mas/yr, sky sidebands dphi2 -25..-13 and +4..+9 (avoid stream 45 at +10..+14), subtract model leakage (report with/without). Statistic R = branch excess / track excess: predicted ~1.0 (25 deg) vs ~0.35 (12.5),

    = 6 sigma apart; pre-register 5-sigma excess, R, CMD shift + mean parallax (4.0-4.6 kpc), pmra gradient along phi1 (strongest discriminant vs field), Gaia RVS v_los at phi1 > 18. Clean bins phi1 6-14. Outcomes: R ~ 1 -> branch real, current score unfair, angle open, score track+branch with a full Gaia selection; R ~ 0.3 -> 12.5 stands with its own weak branch confirmed; no excess -> both models over-produce 250-500 Myr off-track debris -> release history suspect.

    Test 1: selection-aware score (sensitivity bound, not a likelihood)¶

    L_i = [(1-eps) f(w_i|u_i) + eps/|S(u_i)|] / [(1-eps) F_S(u_i) + eps], F_S = integral_S f(w|u); S empirical and fixed before scoring: per 2-deg phi1 bin, +-k MAD around members' medians in (dphi2, pmra, pmdec), k = 2,3,4,6 as a family; F_S as hard particle count first; floor F_S at 10 effective particles; no model-dependent S. Mock check on the 20 T2 catalogues truncated by the same recipe (maximum must stay at 27). Can soften the 12.5 preference, never establish 25.

    Against its own ranking¶

    Test 2 rests on the models' absolute 250-500 Myr debris (release history never varied); a null means "release model wrong", and the leakage correction re-introduces the model. Test 1 is cheaper (hours) and bounds the sensitivity now.
