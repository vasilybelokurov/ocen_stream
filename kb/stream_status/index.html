    Stream 54: status (2026-10-18)¶

    This page summarises the stream work since 2026-10-13. Earlier results are on the PM refinement page (historical). Every ln L below names its score version and particle number. Totals from different versions or particle numbers are not comparable; the versions are defined on stream likelihood versions.

    Summary¶

    • A bar is needed, but the fit is not yet satisfactory. Barred models beat the best axisymmetric model by about 3300–4100 in ln L. No model reproduces the steep μδ of the turn at φ1 > 22°.
    • Two objectives disagree. Fits to the binned tracks halve the track χ2 but lose 100–450 in the star-by-star ln L. The disagreement is a trade-off between the sky track and the PM track in the turn.
    • The original likelihood did not converge with particle number. It is replaced by fixed χ bins with equal weights. A jackknife removes the small-particle-number bias.
    • A 9-parameter emulator is built and validated (470 sprays). It does not yet constrain the bar. One independently checked point beats the paper models by about 600, but the turn is still not fitted.

    • The refitted null runs to the bounds: distance 5.95 kpc (10.4σ from the adopted 5.43 ± 0.05 kpc), PMs 2.6–3.0σ off.
    • Host variants of the null (halo flattening 0.8/1.2, disc scale 0.8/1.2, vc(R0) 220/240 km s−1, McMillan 2017) change ln L by −630 to +370. All remain 3300–4300 below the 16° bar, and none steepens μδ in the turn (−8.3 to −9.0 mas yr−1 at φ1 = 25°, against −11.0 in the data).
    • Barred residuals. μδ is too shallow by 0.8 mas yr−1 at φ1 = 21° and by 1.2–1.8 at 23–27°. Most of the barred gain over the null (about 2900 of 3250–3550) comes from φ1 > 17°.
    • The 16° vs 28° gap shrinks with particle number. Under the pooled score the 28° − 16° difference is −454 at 4,000 epochs and −98 ± 18 at 512,000 (five seeds), and still trending towards zero. The bar angle is therefore not yet constrained by these comparisons.

    Track objective vs star-by-star likelihood¶

    Fits to binned tracks (Δφ2 and PM medians in 2° bins of φ1; bin/streams/fit_track_objective.py) roughly halve the track χ2 of the refined models. Scored star by star (S2, 32,000 epochs, seeds 5 and 6) they are 100–450 worse in ln L.

    The disagreement comes from the turn (bin/streams/plot_objective_disagreement.py):

    • the track-optimised long bar matches the PM medians to φ1 ≈ 25° but puts the turn debris 1.5° below the members in Δφ2 at φ1 = 21–26°;
    • the refined 28° bar has the sky track right at φ1 = 18–24° but PMs that are too shallow.

    No model found so far gets both. Separately, all barred models place the young debris near the cluster about 1° from the observed track at φ1 = 2–8°; the axisymmetric null does not.

    Likelihood: convergence, bias and noise¶

    • Legacy score (S2/S3) does not converge. It gives every χ bin with at least 20 particles equal weight, so the number of bins grows with particle number. Model differences changed sign between 4,000 and 128,000 epochs.
    • Fixed χ bins (chosen 2026-10-17). Bins of width 5 with fixed edges and equal weights, empty bins kept. Equal weight per χ interval keeps the model's density along the stream out of the score, because that density is not reliably modelled. The candidate range is χ = 0–200, which holds 93–96% of the model debris inside the data window; no member loses more than 2 in ln L against the pooled score. The library default is still the pooled score until this range is confirmed.
    • Noise is shot noise. The seed variance of each member's ln L follows 1/neff, the effective number of model particles behind its density, for neff ≳ 3. Neighbouring members share particles, so the noise of the total is 2–3 times the independent-star value. Members with few particles sit in the far turn (φ1 > 22°).
    • Bias: corrected with a jackknife. The log of a noisy density is biased low, by about 45–140 at 256,000 epochs depending on the parameters. A delete-group jackknife (10 interleaved groups; src/ocen_dm/streams/score_bias.py) removes it:
    Calibration point Raw: 256k − 128k Jackknife: 256k − 128k Seed sd at 128k / 256k
    14 (near the centre of the design) +73 ± 34 −10 ± 35 85 / 43
    20 (largest bar amplitude × size) +25 ± 27 −5 ± 32 62 / 43

    Means and seed scatter over 8 independent seeds per resolution, fixed χ bins 0–200. The check is limited to about ±35 by the number of seeds. A second-order (delta-method) correction removes only about half the bias.

    Emulator (2026-10-18)¶

    Data. 470 sprays at 256,000 release epochs over 375 points of the 9-parameter space (bin/streams/training_set.py, bin/streams/stream_batch.py), each scored with fixed χ bins 0–200 and jackknife-corrected: - 256 Sobol design points; - 32 points with two extra independent seeds, for the noise. Each repeat shifts the release-time grid by a random offset as well as redrawing the escape offsets; the original runs agree with the shifted repeats (mean difference −24 ± 20); - two refinement rounds (100 and 60 runs) chosen by predicted ln L + 2σ, with exploration points, held-out points and repeats; - 8 seeds at the emulator maximum.

    Model. Stochastic kriging (src/ocen_dm/streams/emulator.py; Ankenman, Nelson & Staum 2010, 2010OpRes..58..371A; Binois, Gramacy & Ludkovski, arXiv:1611.05902): - a GP for the mean, with each point's noise fixed; - a noise surface ln s² = a + b ln vjk + GP fitted to the repeat variances (median noise sd 65 in ln L).

    Validation (5-fold, noise model refitted in each fold; results/plot_data/emulator_validate_final_20261018.json):

    Results. - The bar is not yet constrained. The emulator's uncertainty is about 575 at the profile peaks and 806 at its optimised maximum, while each one-parameter profile (maximum over the other eight) varies by only 500–1350. Profiles are maxima over a finite candidate set with pointwise bands, not uncertainty-integrated. The emulator maximum moves between fits. - The two paper models are indistinguishable at current precision: 16° −22174 and 28° −22171 (2 seeds each; difference 3 ± ~70). They also differ in distance, amplitude and size, so this is not a clean test of the angle. - One independently replicated point is about 600 better: −21569 ± 11 (8 seeds) at angle 17°, Ωb 32, amplitude 1.57, size 0.89, distance 5.79 kpc, vc 220 km s−1, q 1.02. Its slow bar, large distance and low vc describe this candidate, not a measurement. The score has no prior on ω Cen's distance (adopted 5.43 ± 0.05 kpc). vc sits at the lower edge of the range; the latest emulator maximum also sits at the edges of μδ and halo q. - Predicted maxima are optimistic. The three checked predicted-best points came in 430–770 below their predictions. - The turn is still not fitted. At φ1 = 21° the best point improves the μδ residual from −0.82 to −0.16 mas yr−1 (paper 16°), but its Δφ2 offset grows from 0.47° to 1.42°. This is a remaining mismatch, not shown to be unavoidable everywhere in the space.

    Open decisions: 1. extend vc below 220 km s−1 (and check the μδ and halo-q edges); 2. add the distance measurement as a prior or keep the score likelihood-only; 3. denser local design around the top (a local GP is an experiment), keeping global exploration and fresh held-out points; 4. choose the tempering by held-out segments and mock coverage.
