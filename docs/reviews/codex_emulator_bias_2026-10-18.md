# Codex review: low-angle bias of the emulator in the mock recovery test (2026-10-18)

Codex CLI, effort high, read-only.


The “exactly the real-data pipeline” premise is false: mock recovery adds the true parameters as an optimizer start and optimizes its profiles; the real-data report starts from measured points and constructs profiles from a finite candidate set. This makes the mock search more informed, yet T2 still fails. ([mock_recovery.py:80–111](<bin/streams/mock_recovery.py:80>), [emulator_report.py:75–100](<bin/streams/emulator_report.py:75>))

**My recommendation: retain GPs to guide simulations, but use angle-stratified, multi-start local searches with independently checked profile values. Do not infer bar-angle uncertainty from the current global GP.** T2c1 now confirms T2c0: angle error −15.9°, truth residual 5.92σ. ([T2c1 recovery.json:55–112](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c1_20261018/recovery.json:55>))

The roots, in order of confidence:

1. **Missing high-likelihood joint coverage is demonstrated.** For T2c0, the independent truth check beats every fitted training run by 1,954; for T2c1, by 2,027. Neither catalogue supplies the emulator with observations near the known high score. Marginal angle-bin counts cannot establish coverage of a coupled nine-dimensional ridge. Moreover, 488 sprays mean **375 distinct fitted locations**, 470 fitted runs and 18 held-out runs—not 488 independent locations. ([T2c0 recovery.json:14–64](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c0_20261018/recovery.json:14>), [T2c1 recovery.json:55–64](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c1_20261018/recovery.json:55>), [mock_recovery.py:54–66](<bin/streams/mock_recovery.py:54>))

2. **The acquisition policy can reinforce this gap.** It adds candidates around the ten highest measured scores, then selects by global \(\mu+2\sigma\); batch uncertainty updates keep the fitted kernel fixed. An underestimated alternative ridge can therefore lose both exploitation and uncertainty-driven exploration. The mechanism exists; its causal contribution needs an ablation. ([emulator_design.py:161–193](<bin/streams/emulator_design.py:161>))

3. **Predictive uncertainty fails where it matters; the proposed explanation remains unproved.** T2’s top-quintile coverage is already only 79%, versus 91% globally. “Globally calibrated” conceals a visible failure. The implementation conditions on fitted kernel and noise parameters; it does not propagate their uncertainty. But the results do not distinguish nonstationarity, a poorly aligned kernel metric, mean misspecification or inadequate observations. ([T2c0 recovery.json:17–33](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c0_20261018/recovery.json:17>), [emulator.py:40–79](<src/ocen_dm/streams/emulator.py:40>))

4. **Constant-mean reversion is real, but not an established diagnosis.** The GP subtracts the unweighted mean of distinct-point scores. Recomputing that mean gives −34,136 for T2c0—not approximately −27,000. Its prediction at truth, −27,244, is substantially *above* that mean. Shrinkage may suppress the peak, but “it returned to the ensemble mean” is inaccurate here. ([emulator.py:25–34,68–79](<src/ocen_dm/streams/emulator.py:25>), [T2c0 rescore.json:1](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c0_20261018/rescore.json:1>), [T2c0 recovery.json:55–61](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c0_20261018/recovery.json:55>))

5. **There is a separate mock-target issue.** Catalogues are sampled from one finite spray’s raw KDE, whereas the emulator targets jackknife-corrected scores from independent sprays. Generating-spray self-scores exceed independent checks by roughly 528–741 across these four catalogues. That does not explain away a 5,070-point interpolation error, but it prevents treating the experiment as exact recovery from the infinite-particle target. Both catalogues at a truth also share that generating spray. ([mock_data.py:59–85,99–105](<bin/streams/mock_data.py:59>), [emulator.py:5–6](<src/ocen_dm/streams/emulator.py:5>), [T1c0 recovery.json:55–61](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T1c0_20261018/recovery.json:55>), [T1c1 recovery.json:55–61](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T1c1_20261018/recovery.json:55>), [T2c0 recovery.json:55–61](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c0_20261018/recovery.json:55>), [T2c1 recovery.json:55–61](<results/streams/rescore_mockwide_T2c1_20261018/recovery.json:55>))

**Ranked work programme**

1. **First, benchmark using existing scores.** Compare Sobol-only against the full design, retaining grouped replicates. Use blocked validation by angle–Ω region and joint neighbourhood, alongside random folds. Compare constant, linear and regularized quadratic means with GP residuals; refit everything inside each fold. Score performance on independent truth checks, high-score holdouts and likelihood *differences*, not aggregate RMSE alone. Current CV randomly partitions the existing locations, so it does not test discovery of an omitted ridge. ([emulator_design.py:107–123](<bin/streams/emulator_design.py:107>))

   Also perform an explicitly **oracle diagnostic**: add the already available truth-check observation and refit. If the fitted peak moves sharply, that establishes sensitivity to missing information. It is not an acceptance test.

2. **Spend the next simulation budget on stratified local search.** Start with approximately 100 runs at 128k: 80 distributed across five angle anchors and separated nuisance-parameter starts, plus 20 global discovery runs. Adapt in small batches; share observations between neighbouring angles rather than fitting isolated eight-dimensional GPs to tiny samples. Promote perhaps 20 competitive points to independent 256k checks. At your supplied timings, that is about **14 core-hours**, before replication and fitting.

   Test this first on failing T2 with the same budget and stopping rules intended for real data, without truth-informed starts. Local trust-region BO is a defensible approach to heterogeneous objectives; it is not a guarantee of finding every mode. ([TuRBO paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/file/6c990b7aca7bc7058f5e98ea909e924b-Paper.pdf))

3. **Use quadratic means as a cheap challenger, not the main cure.** A full quadratic has 55 coefficients in nine dimensions: regularize it and include coefficient uncertainty. It can describe a rotated broad ridge; it cannot establish an unobserved narrow peak. Polynomial means plus GP residuals have a standard formulation. ([GPML, §2.7](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW2.pdf))

4. **Defer blanket densification and hard partitions.** Another 300–500 runs at 128k costs 30–50 core-hours. Under uniform nine-dimensional spacing, that improves nominal spacing by only about 6–9% relative to 375 locations. Prefer a targeted pilot with measurable improvement before buying that expansion.

Before mixing resolutions, calibrate 128k versus 256k **at newly found competitive points**. Existing documented convergence checks cover two calibration locations and have uncertainty around 35; they do not establish resolution equivalence on every mock ridge. The current loader also explicitly rejects non-256k runs, so mixed-resolution fitting requires a deliberate change. ([stream_status.md:73–79](<docs/kb/stream_status.md:73>), [emulator_design.py:51–57](<bin/streams/emulator_design.py:51>))

**Acceptance criteria**

- **Search:** the independently checked best candidate must not score materially below the known truth check. Use a predeclared search tolerance—say 100 log-score units initially—and replicate enough to resolve that comparison. This is necessary, not sufficient.
- **Surrogate:** validate profile *differences* near competing modes and interval boundaries. For inference using \(\exp(\ell/T)\), target numerical errors well below \(T\), for example \(0.2T\). If unattainable at \(T=1\), precise raw-likelihood inference is unsupported. Tempering needs statistical justification, not selection to conceal emulator error.
- **Recovery:** test angles 12/20/27/33, weak bars and nuisance placements away from the explored ridge. Compare against independently evaluated optima; do not require the finite-sample optimum to equal the generating parameters.
- **Coverage:** use repeated catalogues and independent generating sprays. Assess nominal interval coverage with binomial uncertainty, separately by truth regime. Weak-bar tests may correctly return broad angle intervals. Neither two catalogues nor a pointwise GP band establishes parameter coverage; current profile bands are simply the GP standard deviation at each selected maximizer. ([mock_recovery.py:109–112](<bin/streams/mock_recovery.py:109>))
- **Pipeline integrity:** verify catalogue hashes and settings before recovery. The current recovery script reads the supplied mock and rescore independently without checking their association, and overwrites `recovery.json`. Fix that before expanding the campaign. ([mock_recovery.py:51–55,117](<bin/streams/mock_recovery.py:51>))

A GP is suitable as a **search model** here. Nine dimensions alone do not disqualify it. For the immediate angle question, sequential BO within angle strata is preferable to MCMC: sampling the current surrogate would faithfully reproduce its missed peak. Direct-spray MCMC is expensive, and exponentiating an approximately unbiased **log** score does not produce the unbiased likelihood estimator required for exact pseudo-marginal sampling. The correction currently operates on log totals. ([score.py:419–422](<src/ocen_dm/streams/score.py:419>), [Andrieu & Roberts](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.5480))

1. **Verified versus assumed.** I read the requested implementation and four completed recovery files, and recomputed grouped counts, means and simulation-identity checks from stored scores. Their catalogue hashes match. I did not rerun GP fits or tests. The emulator unit tests exercise a smooth quadratic toy and broad CV coverage, not narrow-ridge discovery or end-to-end parameter recovery. Cutoff/jackknife tests establish agreement between scoring implementations, not recovery correctness. The efficiency of local BO and the precise ridge geometry remain assumptions to test. ([test_emulator.py:8–41](<tests/test_emulator.py:8>), [test_score_cutoff.py:29–47](<tests/test_score_cutoff.py:29>))

2. **Strongest argument against my recommendation.** Local searches can miss disconnected modes, and angle stratification still leaves eight nuisance dimensions. A regularized global trend could exploit structure more efficiently and make the existing library adequate with little additional simulation. My recommendation wins only if matched-budget mock experiments demonstrate better independently checked regret and coverage.

3. **Needed for confidence.** Matched-budget design/kernel ablations; direct scores at reported emulator maxima; convergence checks on competitive mock points; independent generating-spray repeats; identical blinded recovery/reporting procedures; and validated physical-coordinate profiles. In particular, the current “size” profile fixes the unit coordinate while physical size changes with Ω, \(v_c\) and \(q\), so it is not a fixed-physical-size profile. ([mock_recovery.py:98–109](<bin/streams/mock_recovery.py:98>), [emulator_design.py:36–39](<bin/streams/emulator_design.py:36>))
