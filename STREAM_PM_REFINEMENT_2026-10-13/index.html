    Stream proper motions and the bar angle: renewed investigation¶

    Historical (2026-10-13)

    Current status: kb/stream_status.md. All ln L values on this page are the legacy score S2 (equal-weight chi bins, implied background widths; see kb/models/stream_likelihood_versions.md), at 32,000 release epochs (pilots at 8,000). They are not comparable with later scores.

    The task is to reproduce the remaining stream-54 proper-motion structure and determine whether the apparent preference for a low bar angle survives joint refitting. This note will record findings, including negative results.

    First experiment: complete the nuisance refinement¶

    The previous broad six-dimensional search found comparable maxima at 16 and 28 degrees, but was not locally refined. Other angle comparisons fixed distance, proper motions, or bar size. Those comparisons do not establish the profiled angle preference.

    bin/streams/refine_bar_profiles.py refines both angles with the original conditional score. Pattern speed, amplitude, bar size, cluster distance, and both cluster proper motions move together. Coordinates are scaled before bounded COBYQA optimization. The initial candidates include the best saved Sobol points and the best relevant grid models. Every evaluation and spray is saved; the result file is replaced atomically. Existing output is preserved.

    The distance measurement is 5.43 +/- 0.05 kpc, and each PM measurement has an uncertainty of 0.027 mas/yr. These are separate penalty terms. Bar length is bounded by corotation using the measured Hunter A2 half-maximum length of 4.25 kpc. The 5 kpc definition remains an option. Bounds and measurements are recorded in the output. There is no new angular prior or error inflation.

    The search uses 8,000 release epochs and seed 1. Candidate improvement will be checked at 32,000 epochs with independent seeds 4, 5, and 6. Comparisons are made only at equal particle count. This is a diagnostic refinement; an optimization budget ending before convergence is reported as such, and held-out seed checks do not substitute for mock calibration.

    CPU limit approved by the user: two cores. Run each angle in a separate single-thread process, with BLAS also limited to one thread. Expected first refinement runtime: 15-30 minutes, to be updated from measured timings.

    Interpretation requirements¶

    A better raw score is insufficient. Compare the actual sky and PM tracks, the remaining PM residuals, the external distance/PM tension, the nuisance boundaries, and scores from independent seeds. Keep the near-cluster and distal segments visible. Do not claim that a prior forcing a normal bar angle has explained the low-angle preference.

    Possible further diagnostics include projecting PM residuals along and across the measured sky track and testing whether the stars with radial velocities can reach physically plausible escape locations in the barred host. These are distinct from comparing a star orbit with the cluster orbit using arbitrary position/velocity scales.

    PM geometry diagnostic¶

    bin/streams/diagnose_pm_profiles.py predicts PMs with particles weighted only by their agreement with each member's sky position. It retains the existing chi-bin weights, and never uses an observed PM to select a model particle. The diagnostic plot uses a declared two-degree tube around the measured ridge; the fit still uses all members. Each bin records its member count and model PM/distance percentiles. This avoids attributing a change in broad distribution shape to a shift of the ridge.

    The initial diagnostic, before the local fits finished, suggests two different errors. Around phi1=11-17 degrees, the PM vector direction differs by several degrees. At the distal knee, phi1=23-27, the vector direction is much closer, but its magnitude is too small. A purely local distance rescaling at fixed relative space velocity would remove most of that distal PM residual. This is a decomposition of the error, not a measured distance or a new freedom in the dynamical model: changing the actual cluster distance also changes the orbit, and must be forward-modelled.

    Separate physical hypothesis: spiral arms¶

    The stream host omits the spiral potential supplied with the local Hunter model. The published model contains independently rotating bar and spiral patterns. The reference spiral speed is 22.5 km/s/kpc and the reported test range is 17.5-25. The original setup uses both components at phase -25 degrees today, in a frame with Sun at negative x.

    Sources: Hunter et al. 2024, Sections 2.1.6 and 2.2.2, and the authors' AGAMA implementation. The local exported potential and construction script are the numerical inputs; they are not assumed to equal every parameter in the published text.

    src/ocen_dm/streams/spiral.py reflects the exported CylSpline tables into this project's x_model=-x_source frame by applying the exact real-Fourier parity to potential and derivative coefficients. It then adds independent positive pattern rotation. A sign change of pattern speed alone would leave the spiral winding wrong. Original and transformed hashes are saved under results/streams/spiral_potential/.

    bin/streams/diagnose_spiral_forcing.py check verifies force/potential reflection, zero-amplitude identity, force addition, and past/present phase. The reflection force error is 9.5e-16 relative to the largest checked spiral force. Phase/addition errors are below 4e-14. The source has a numerical azimuthal-monopole remainder of 1.17e-7 of the spiral potential amplitude; reflection preserves it to 8e-17. An initial absolute-zero threshold rejected that source remainder; the check now verifies preservation of the source monopole explicitly. No measured-data errors or likelihood widths changed.

    The pilot is a paired on/off experiment at fixed cluster/bar parameters, fixed present spiral phase 25 degrees, and a fresh spray seed. A source snapshot is frozen before each pilot. A gain in one seed is only a lead: check the PM tracks, independent seeds, and nuisance refitting before interpreting it as an explanation for the angle preference.

    First numerical findings¶

    The exploratory local searches were stopped after 19 evaluations at 16 degrees and 28 at 28, when an independent 8,000-epoch seed shift exceeded the residual angle gap. They are not converged profile maxima. Their checkpoints and every evaluated spray remain available. Three independent 32,000-epoch sprays (seeds 4, 5, 6) were run for each selected candidate and its initial reference.

    Angle Initial mean ln L Refined mean ln L Gain
    16 deg -22394.7 -22178.6 +216.1
    28 deg -22957.9 -22463.6 +494.3

    The paired 16-minus-28 differences are 314.5, 172.4, and 368.1: mean 285.0, seed scatter 101.0. These quantify simulation noise at two candidates; they are not observational errors or a significance for the bar angle. The 28-degree candidate compensates with D=5.801 kpc and pmdec=-6.8256 mas/yr, near the imposed -3-sigma PM boundary. Its distance is 7.4 sigma above the adopted 5.43 +/- 0.05 measurement (2.5 sigma under the older stream prior of +/- 0.15). This is unresolved external-measurement tension, not a successful removal of the angle problem.

    The fixed-parameter spiral pilot at 28 degrees, seed 4, improves ln L by 277.1 (fiducial) or 317.2 (fast), versus 41.0 for the slow pattern. The gain is mostly distal. Its sky-conditioned PMDEC shifts are only about 0.1-0.25 mas/yr; a 1-2 mas/yr residual remains in the distal bins. This pilot does not solve either requested problem and still needs denser, independent seeds before treating its gain as reliable.

    Likelihood density audit¶

    Writing the implemented score as

    L(w|u) = pi(u) [f(u,w)/f_u(u)] + [1-pi(u)] g_w(w)

    gives pi(u) = (1-eps) f_u / [(1-eps) f_u + eps g_u]. The foreground conditional is density-free, but its mixture weight is not. This is an intentional coverage behavior in the original formula and should not be described as complete independence from the model's along-stream density.

    bin/streams/audit_conditional_density.py isolates that dependency by rescoring with a fixed conditional mixing weight, while retaining all kernels and background constants. Production scoring is unchanged; return_components=True only exposes densities for this audit. Every saved validation/pilot score replays with zero difference.

    The change does not remove the low-angle ranking. In the original 56-candidate grid the best 28-degree deficit changes from 1447.3 to 1443.9. In the independent refined candidates it strengthens the average 16-degree advantage from 285.0 to about 392.0. The density mixing issue is real, but is not an explanation of the angle preference in these tests. The original background-support normalization issue remains separate. (Update: fixed on 2026-10-17, score version S3; see kb/models/stream_likelihood_versions.md.)

    RV-star connection diagnostic¶

    bin/streams/backtrack_barred_release.py integrates the RV stars backward in the actual fitted barred host plus the moving progenitor potential. It scans distances and compares encounters in units of the model's time-dependent tidal radius and release velocity. Model stars chosen by sky position provide a reference check. It includes a small number of observable-error draws, and records edge hits. This is a diagnostic at two fitted states, not a complete ancestry exclusion or posterior.

    This addresses a question the earlier axisymmetric backtrack could not: whether the measured PM/RV combinations can originate near the cluster in the model that is actually being fitted, rather than whether another optimizer can improve an uncalibrated scalar score.

    Completed: 29 RV stars plus 29 sky-matched model controls at each angle. For phi1<17, the median nominal encounter diagnostic is 0.80 for the data at either angle, versus 1.05/0.99 for model controls. For the 13 knee RV stars, data medians are 1.99/1.86 at 16/28 degrees, versus 1.11/1.13 for controls. Selecting the best of four small observational-error draws gives 1.96/1.76 for the knee data. Some distal stars have relative velocities of several release-velocity scales even at their best encounter.

    This identifies a physical mismatch in the tested recent fixed-potential models beyond the scoring choice. It does not exclude ancestry: the distance grid is coarse, error sampling is sparse, only two cluster states/bar histories are tested, and the encounter metric is not a release likelihood.

    Escape-law pilot¶

    Run at 8,000 release epochs, seed 4, legacy score S2 (results/plot_data/escape_pilot_20261013/).

    The current Fardal/Gala release has zero relative radial motion. Its implementation matches the local AGAMA tutorial, so this is not a sign or transcription bug. Chen et al. 2025 provide a calibrated alternative with radial escape motion, correlated escape radius/direction, and integration in the progenitor potential. The code uses their five-dimensional Gaussian prescription and the actual spherical satellite escape speed. This is a controlled alternative; Chen's tests do not certify it for Omega Cen's massive, central, barred orbit.

    src/ocen_dm/streams/escape.py implements the new prescription. The Fardal default is bitwise identical to the committed reference; Chen isolated escape energies close to zero to 2.8e-15 relative precision, and sample moments/correlation agree with the stated parameters. Spin is not silently added to the new calibrated prescription.

    bin/streams/compare_escape_recipes.py makes a paired comparison of release law and launch-arm selection. Both launch arms can contribute positive Gibbons chi after integration, so filtering on the initial outer-arm label is an additional assumption. The four pilot cases isolate these effects; the old default is preserved. All bar/cluster parameters and likelihood kernels stay fixed; new-case metadata and source hashes are recorded.

    The fixed-state, seed-4 pilot does not fix the mismatch. At 28 degrees, Fardal -> Chen increases the sky-conditioned PM-ridge RMS from 1.44 to 1.51 mas/yr and lowers ln L by 465. Including both launch arms adds about 200 positive-chi particles to Fardal (~4%) and only 50 to Chen; its score changes are small and its PM shifts do not resolve the residual. The outer-arm subset from the both-arm Fardal run matches the default in every saved observable and chi/age array exactly. These are tests at frozen Fardal-fitted states, not a rejection of every refitted Chen model.

    Next connection controls: extend the backward interval to 2 Gyr while retaining the same present state/potential. The 700 Myr foreground cut is a modelling assumption, not an age measurement for the mismatched knee. If needed, the script also supports the existing slowing-bar prescription and the actual catalogue cluster state, with independently generated model controls. These are distinct tests, not a change to the active likelihood.

    Update: the 2 Gyr backward run exists (results/plot_data/bar_profile_refinement_20261013/barred_backtrack_an{16,28}_b2000.json) but was never analysed.
